# 8-Week Study Plan

About 6 to 8 hours per week. Adjust the pace to your interview date.

| Week | Focus | Read | Do |
|------|-------|------|----|
| 1 | Method and numbers | [00 Framework](../docs/00-interview-framework.md), [06 Estimation](../docs/06-estimation-and-metrics.md) | Memorize the latency and size tables. Do 5 estimations for apps you use. |
| 2 | Scaling and caching | [01 Scaling](../docs/01-scaling-and-traffic.md) | Explain each caching pattern out loud. Answer the module questions. |
| 3 | Data storage | [02 Data storage](../docs/02-data-storage.md) | Pick a shard key for 3 different apps and defend it. |
| 4 | Communication | [04 Communication](../docs/04-communication.md) | Solve the [URL shortener](../case-studies/url-shortener.md) from a blank template, then compare. |
| 5 | Consistency | [03 Consistency](../docs/03-consistency-and-distributed-data.md) | Label each part of a design as CP or AP with a reason. Write a Saga for checkout. |
| 6 | Reliability | [05 Reliability](../docs/05-reliability-and-protection.md) | Solve the [rate limiter](../case-studies/rate-limiter.md). Design a retry policy. |
| 7 | Observability, security, mixed practice | [07 Observability and security](../docs/07-observability-and-security.md) | Do 2 new problems from the [practice list](../case-studies/README.md), timed at 45 min. |
| 8 | Mock interviews | All modules, quick revision | 3 to 4 full mock interviews with a friend or recorded solo. Review mistakes. |

## Weekly routine

- 2 sessions reading a module and writing a one-page summary in your own words
- 1 session answering the interview questions out loud
- 1 session on a practice problem with the template

## Readiness checklist

- [ ] I can run the 7-step framework without notes
- [ ] I can estimate QPS and storage in 3 minutes
- [ ] I can compare SQL and NoSQL, REST and gRPC, 2PC and Saga without hesitating
- [ ] I name failure modes unprompted
- [ ] I state tradeoffs as "X because Y, at the cost of Z"
- [ ] I have completed at least 8 full practice designs
